About the solver
The numerical solver remains a 2D D2Q9 approximation. The airflow panel renders spatial 3D streamlines around the full STL using a simplified surface-deflection model; this is not a validated 3D CFD solver.
All mesh processing and solving are local to this browser. For orientation changes, click Apply rotation and then Solve airflow. Camera orbit and display toggles do not trigger solver runs.

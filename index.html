About the solver
2D D2Q9 lattice-Boltzmann (BGK) flow solver using the STL's X–Z projected triangle mask. Solver runs on demand, not during camera motion. This is a low-order visualization tool, not validated 3D CFD: the mesh projection, turbulence, internal geometry, and forces are not engineering-accurate.
All mesh processing and solving are local to this browser. For orientation changes, click Apply rotation and then Solve airflow. Camera orbit and display toggles do not trigger solver runs.
